Date Functions

Date Manipulation and Extraction

PostgreSQL date functions extract components from dates, calculate differences, and manipulate temporal data for healthcare analytics.

💡 Healthcare Applications: Calculate patient ages, analyze admission patterns by season, determine length of stay, and track disease progression over time.

EXTRACT

Basic

Extract specific components from dates (year, month, day, quarter)

EXTRACT(YEAR FROM date)
EXTRACT(MONTH FROM date)
EXTRACT(QUARTER FROM date)

DATE_PART

Basic

Alternative syntax for extracting date components

DATE_PART('year', date)
DATE_PART('month', date)

AGE

Intermediate

Calculate age between two dates or from birth date

AGE(date_of_birth)
AGE(end_date, start_date)

DATE_TRUNC

Intermediate

Truncate date to specified precision

DATE_TRUNC('month', date)
DATE_TRUNC('week', date)

Time Functions

Time Manipulation and Timezone Handling

PostgreSQL time functions handle time extraction, timezone conversions, and temporal calculations for precise healthcare timing.

💡 Healthcare Applications: Medication administration timing, lab result turnaround times, multi-timezone telemedicine coordination, and clinical event sequencing.

EXTRACT (Time)

Basic

Extract time components (hour, minute, second)

EXTRACT(HOUR FROM timestamp)
EXTRACT(MINUTE FROM timestamp)

AT TIME ZONE

Intermediate

Convert between timezones

timestamp AT TIME ZONE 'UTC'
timestamp AT TIME ZONE 'America/New_York'

CURRENT_TIMESTAMP

Basic

Get current date and time with timezone

CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
LOCALTIMESTAMP

TIMEZONE

Intermediate

Convert to specific timezone

TIMEZONE('America/New_York', timestamp)

Interval Calculations

Time Interval Calculations

PostgreSQL interval functions calculate durations, add/subtract time periods, and manage temporal relationships in healthcare workflows.

💡 Healthcare Applications: Calculate length of stay, determine readmission windows, schedule follow-up appointments, and analyze treatment durations.

Interval Arithmetic

Basic

Add/subtract intervals from dates

date + INTERVAL '7 days'
timestamp - INTERVAL '2 hours'

Date Subtraction

Basic

Calculate difference between dates

date2 - date1
AGE(date2, date1)

Generate Series

Advanced

Generate time series for reporting

GENERATE_SERIES(start, end, interval)

OVERLAPS

Advanced

Check for temporal overlaps

(start1, end1) OVERLAPS (start2, end2)
