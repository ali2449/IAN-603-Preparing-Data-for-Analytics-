Joins + reshaping
A join can change the fact count before the reshape
Safe pattern
Join a one-row-per-entity dimension to fact rows without multiplying facts.
Risky pattern
Join two one-to-many child tables and create combinations between them.
Result
Counts and sums can become inflated before the pivot even starts.

Reveal key ideas

Closure
The reshaping habits to remember
1
State grain first
Know what one row means before and after reshaping.
2
Pivot deliberately
Conditional aggregation turns selected category values into columns.
3
Unpivot deliberately
UNION ALL and LATERAL VALUES turn repeated columns into rows.
4
Protect NULL meaning
Missing and zero are different analytical states.
5
Validate totals
Reshaping can hide join mistakes or aggregation loss.
6
Choose shape for use
Long is flexible; wide is useful for stable matrices.
