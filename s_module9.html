IAN 603 · Module 9 · Slide 1
Start here

Data cleaning is evidence-based decision making

Observe

Profile what is actually present

Count missing, blank, invalid, duplicate, and unusual values before changing anything.

Decide

Apply documented rules

Standardize representation only where the intended meaning is known.

Preserve

Keep source evidence

A cleaned field is an interpretation of the raw value, not a replacement for provenance.

Review

Do not guess unknown facts

Questionable rows may need quarantine or human review rather than automatic repair.

IAN 603 · Module 9 · Slide 2
Start here

Invalid does not mean “replace with something plausible”

Bad example

A price is negative, so replace it with ABS(price).

Better response

Flag the negative price as invalid and investigate the source or business rule.

WhyABS changes the value without proving what the correct price should be.

A clean candidate is not the same as a verified truth

Passes the selected technical checks for this exercise.
Review

IAN 603 · Module 9 · Slide 55
Assessment
Can you explain the cleaning decisions?
NULL vs blank
Why are they different SQL states?
Invalid numeric
Why flag before correction?
Duplicates
Why separate detection from survivor selection?
Regex
What does a shape check prove and not prove?
Transactions
Why use UPDATE RETURNING with rollback while testing?
Reveal key ideas
NULL and blank are different values. Invalid data does not reveal the correct replacement. Duplicate detection identifies candidate groups while survivor rules are separate policy decisions. Regex proves only the selected pattern. Transactions and RETURNING let you inspect changes before keeping them.

IAN 603 · Module 9 · Slide 57
Closure
The cleaning habits to keep
1
Profile first
Measure problems before changing data.
2
Preserve uncertainty
Do not invent replacements for unknown facts.
3
Separate tasks
Standardization, validation, deduplication, and correction are different.
4
Use safe layers
Views, quarantine sets, transactions, and lineage improve reproducibility.
5
Prevent later
Add constraints after stable rules and legacy issues are understood.
6
Measure quality openly
Keep component dimensions visible even when using a summary score.
