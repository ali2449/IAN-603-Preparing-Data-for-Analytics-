Course Overview

The Preparing Data for Analytics course provides a practical foundation in managing, cleaning, transforming, and organizing data for analytical use. Students work with structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data while developing the skills needed to prepare reliable datasets for analysis.

Through 14 comprehensive modules, students develop skills in SQL and Spark SQL while working with real-world datasets and techniques such as data cleaning, normalization, joins, transformations, and advanced querying. The course supports both beginners and learners with prior programming experience and emphasizes practical data preparation workflows used in modern analytics environments.
